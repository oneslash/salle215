A native iPhone and iPad app for Langfuse. Check what’s failing, open the trace, rate the answer and compare prompts, wherever you are.

Download on the App Store Read the guide

iOS 26 or later. Langfuse Cloud and self-hosted Langfuse v4. Try the built-in sample project without an account.

Everything you check on the Langfuse dashboard, on your phone.

Vitals with context

Error rate, P95 latency, spend and answer quality, each against its usual range. Scrub a chart to read any point.

Traces that explain themselves

Read the question, inspect failures and follow the shared-axis timeline. More traces load as you scroll.

Review queue

Rate the answer right below it. Open sources when you need them. Connected ratings save as Langfuse scores.

Feedback in two taps

Pick reasons, add a note, save. Skip and undo are always there. Existing reviews are never overwritten.

Prompt diffs

Production and staging side by side, with a sentence-level diff of every message.

Search

Paste a trace ID, session, user or error text. Results respect your time range and environment.

Direct to your Langfuse project. Nothing in between.

  • Works with Langfuse Cloud (EU and US) and self-hosted Langfuse v4 over HTTPS.
  • API keys live in the iOS Keychain on your device. Disconnecting removes them.
  • No account, no proxy, no analytics.
  • Reports are cached and rate limits respected. Missing data is shown as unavailable, never as a fake zero.
